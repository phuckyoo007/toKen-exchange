Common questions

I forgot my password. Can you reset it?

We can't reset it for you — your password only unlocks a copy of your encrypted wallet stored on your own device, and we never receive it. If you still have your recovery (seed) phrase, choose "Reset wallet instead" on the unlock screen and re-import using that phrase to set a new password. If you don't have your seed phrase, unfortunately there is no way to recover that wallet; this is the tradeoff of a non-custodial wallet.

A transaction hasn't shown up / seems stuck.

Token Exchange broadcasts your transaction to the public blockchain network you selected; confirmation times and any delay are determined by that network, not by us. Check the transaction's hash on the appropriate block explorer for the network you used to see its live status.

Buy or Sell isn't working / my payment is stuck.

Buy and Sell are handled by our third-party partners, Coinbase and Onramper. If a payment, verification (KYC), or payout issue comes up during a Buy or Sell, it needs to be resolved with that provider directly, since they process the payment and hold the relevant account details — not us.

Swap says it isn't available on this network.

Swap only enables once a verified router is configured for the network you're on. If you expect swaps to work here, double-check your network selection in Settings, or let us know which network you'd like to see supported.

I found a bug or have a feature idea.

Email us the details (and a screenshot, if it's visual) — we read every message. You can also use "Request a feature" in the Settings screen of the wallet itself.
